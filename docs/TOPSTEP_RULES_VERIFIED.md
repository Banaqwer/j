### 1.2 Rule and objectives (exact official text) [PARAMS] (conf 0.98)
- Rule: "Do not let your account balance hit or go below the Maximum Loss Limit (MLL)".
- Objective 1: "Reach and maintain your Profit Target". Objective 2: "Meet the Consistency Target — your best single day should stay below 55% of your Profit Target to avoid increasing your Consistency Target."
- Single-phase ("one rule and two objectives"); no multi-step structure for the standard Combine; account prefixes 50KTC/100KTC/150KTC (50KTC confirmed; others inferred, conf 0.8).

### 1.3 MLL mechanics [MLL] (conf 0.97)
- End-of-day trailing: "rises as your end-of-day balance grows, but never moves down"; updated at EOD, monitored in real time; realized + unrealized P&L count. Labs FAQ [LABS]: trails the end-of-day closing balance, not intraday peaks (conf 0.95).
- Example: start $50,000/MLL $48,000; +$500 day 1 -> $50,500/$48,500; -$500 day 2 -> $50,000, MLL stays $48,500.
- Locks permanently once it reaches the starting balance. No "+$100" offset exists (conf 0.95).
- Breach = unrealized Net P&L touches limit at any moment; liquidation by market order; a final realized balance above the limit does not undo the breach (example: $47,750 unrealized, $48,050 realized = still a breach). Same logic for DLL, PDLL, PDPT.
- Consequence: liquidated for the rest of the day, ineligible for funding until Reset; practice trading continues; subscription keeps rebilling [SUBS] (conf 0.97). MLL "cannot be adjusted or changed. Topstep makes no exceptions."

### 1.4 Consistency Target [CONS] (conf 0.97)
- "55% is a hard line. It is not rounded, and there is no buffer" (old rule: 50% with hidden buffer). Formula: Best Day Profit ÷ Total Profit; if exceeded, new Profit Target = Best Day ÷ 0.55 (e.g. $1,800 -> $3,273; later $2,200 best day -> $4,000). Losses never reset the best day; applies regardless of current balance.
- Best day updates intraday and locks at 3:10 PM CT; remaining profit must be earned on a separate trading day (same-day trading cannot cure). Pass possible in as few as 2 days; one day impossible [PARAMS] (conf 0.96). Wording differs across pages (see Uncertainties).

### 1.5 Daily Loss Limit [DLL] (conf 0.96)
- Optional in Combine/XFA (automatic in LFA; mandatory for RTP traders, Labs, Pro). Checkout DLL is fixed and carries into the XFA. Trigger: Net P&L hits DLL during 5 PM–3:10 PM CT; "not a rule violation — it's a forced break": flatten, cancel orders, no new trades until 5 PM CT next session (Payout Policy calls this a "Temporary Violation"). Personal DLL / Trailing PDLL / Personal Daily Profit Target settable in TopstepX (Do Nothing / Liquidate / Liquidate and Block).
- Incentives: Responsible Trading Discount $10/$20/$30 per month on NAF Combines, $50 off Back2Funded [PRICE] (conf 0.95); double XFA payout caps if DLL added at checkout on a new Combine bought on/after 2026-06-02 3:30 PM CT [PAYOUT, DCTC] (conf 0.95).

### 1.6 Position limits [PARAMS, SCALE, RISKADJ] (conf 0.95)
Fixed 5/10/15 minis (no Scaling Plan in the Combine). SIL = 5:1 vs SI and counts as 2 micros; MBT/MET capped at mini-equivalents. Overages corrected within 10 seconds ignored; 10+ seconds may trigger review. Product caps above (SI/HG/PL = 0) sit below the account cap and are "temporary, product-specific, actively monitored".

### 1.7 Time rules [PARAMS, HOURS, SUBS] (conf 0.97)
Trading day 5:00 PM CT -> 3:10 PM CT next day (post-5 PM trades count toward next day). All positions flat by 3:10 PM CT (Risk Managers begin flattening 3:08 PM CT; auto-cancel 3:10). No overnight/swing. Sunday open 5 PM CT; Friday close 3:10 PM CT. Holiday early closes: flat 15 minutes before (https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/13350348-topstep-holiday-trading-hours, conf 0.93). No time limit; subscription rebills every 30 days from sign-up; cannot pause; cancellation irreversible.

### 1.9 Resets and Reset Credits [RESET, RLIM] (conf 0.96)
Reset restores balance, MLL, Consistency Target and trading days to day one; pushes rebill 30 days from Reset; limit 2 Resets per account per calendar day; no limit on Combine purchases. Each rebill adds 1 Reset Credit (size- and type-specific: Standard/NAF/DLL; non-transferable; cannot combine; credits issued on/after 2025-12-11 expire after 1 year; oldest first). Resets disabled during failed-payment retry (2 retries) [SUBS]. Resets/credits non-refundable [REFUND].

### 1.10 After pass / multiple accounts [PARAMS, XFA, XACT] (conf 0.96)
Pass -> account closes, subscription auto-cancels; XFA starts at $0 (profits do not carry over); status up to 30 minutes; Friday pass trades Sunday 5 PM CT. Activation blocked if 5 active XFAs or an LFA exists. No limit on simultaneous Combines; all under one profile (multiple profiles violate Terms). Automation allowed "with conditions" (no support/exceptions) [PARAMS]; orders must originate from personal device, no VPS/VPN [API].
