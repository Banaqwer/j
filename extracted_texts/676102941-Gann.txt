ATM Trade
1 Money is made in selling options not 
by buying options.
2 Look for just in the money or at the 
money options.
3 After the first 1-5 min., draw nearest 
perfect Gann square horizontal lines 
on the chart.
4 Look for a combination of perfect 
Gann square & patterns.

5 Look for a setup with Small SL 5-7 pts.
6 Trade only those setups which have 
got small sl.
7 Minimum risk reward is 1:2.5/3.
   Book 50% at RR & trail SL for balance 
as per Gann level.
8 Use a 30/36 period moving average for 
trading with a directional bias. 
9 SAR is SAAR(Gist) of ATM trades.
