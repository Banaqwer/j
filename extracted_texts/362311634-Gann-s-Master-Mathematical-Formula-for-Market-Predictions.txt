market predictions just to get money, which we do not need or have to have; we will only sell the

man or woman cannot make a success investing or trading we will refuse to teach them the 
courses”.  “The Master Calculator can only be used with the Master Courses. It is not sold 
separate from the other courses”.  The price shown for Gann’s Master Course in this ad is 
$5,000.00, it does not list the additional cost of the Master Mathematical Calculator. The ad 
describes the calculator as a plastic overlay that accurately measures Time, Price and Trend. 
Many Gann experts and students have tried to use these overlays, which require the chart to be

overlays on price charts but that is still not going to help most people because it turns out that

these same mathematical rules apply to price as well but we will leave this topic for latter. The 
advertisement itself contains clues about the calculator, such as “W.D. Gann’s Record for 52

proved to my entire satisfaction that history repeats and that when we know the past, we can 
determine the future of prices”. The calculator that Gann is talking about in this ad is his

Moving on to the subject of time we know that Gann placed great emphasis on the 
cardinal points of the solar year. These are the Vernal Equinox, Summer Solstice, Autumnal 
Equinox and Winter Solstice or March 21st, June 21st, September 21st, and December 21st
respectively.  Gann always said that the year begins at March 21st (not January 1st) and that this 
was a very important seasonal time.  Now this is where Gann’s “within the circle forms the 
square” quote gets real interesting! If we treat each natural year (March 21st to March 21st) as a 
complete circle, which it is because the earth has gone 360-degrees around the Sun, and take

unique geometrical information about time and squares, i.e. Gann’s plastic overlays. Lets build

both price and time at $50 on the Autumnal Equinox or September 21st. If we bisect this square

the Autumn Equinox we can create four smaller squares with half of the x already completed. If 
we complete these smaller boxes with the missing 45-degree line we will find that these points
